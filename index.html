Built by people who fish.

Taro Resto opened in 2011 as a single counter behind a fish market, run by a family who'd spent three generations on boats before they ever ran a kitchen. The menu still follows the same rule it did on day one: cook what came in today.

We work directly with a handful of local boats, which means smaller catches, shorter menus some weeks, and seafood that never sat in a freezer.
